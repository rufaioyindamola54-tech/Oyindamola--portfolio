## 1. Telecom Customer Churn

**Business question:** Which customer segments show higher churn, and where could retention efforts be focused?

- **Dataset:** 7,200 synthetic customer records; 2,924 churned accounts (40.6%).
- **Analysis:** Compared churn across contract type, tenure, services, and payment method. Built a transparent, rule-based risk review to help prioritize follow-up.
- **Outcome:** Identified segment-level retention priorities and practical opportunities for earlier customer engagement.
- **Tools:** Excel, Power BI.
- **Project files:** [Download the complete case study](./telecom-churn-project.zip).

## 2. Hospital Operations & Experience

**Business question:** Where do waiting-time bottlenecks appear, and how do operational patterns relate to patient experience?

- **Dataset:** 6,500 synthetic visit records.
- **Analysis:** Reviewed waiting times by department and shift, patient satisfaction patterns, and a generated readmission indicator.
- **Selected results:** 73-minute average wait; 67.7% of visits waited more than 60 minutes; 4.02/5 average satisfaction.
- **Outcome:** Highlighted areas for operational review, including long waits by department and shift.
- **Tools:** Excel, Power BI.
- **Project files:** [Download the complete case study](./hospital-operations-project.zip).

## 3. Retail Profitability

**Business question:** How do product, category, region, channel, and discount patterns relate to sales and profit?

- **Dataset:** 8,000 synthetic order lines.
- **Analysis:** Compared sales and profitability across product and market segments, examined discounting and margin, and reviewed returned orders.
- **Selected results:** 3.44 million illustrative sales units; 31.1% dataset profit margin; 5.9% returned orders.
- **Outcome:** Outlined where to review discount decisions and segment-level profitability.
- **Tools:** Excel, Power BI.
- **Project files:** [Download the complete case study](./retail-profitability-project.zip).

## Methods and deliverables

The projects demonstrate a consistent workflow: prepare and validate data, define useful measures, compare business segments, summarize findings, and communicate recommendations. Each ZIP includes the project report, cleaned dataset, data dictionary, Excel analysis workbook, Power BI DAX measures, and dashboard build guide.
